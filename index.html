Brokers

    Factoring context. WEX buys freight invoices from carriers and collects payment directly from the broker, shipper, or forwarder named on each invoice — the carrier is typically advanced funds quickly, well ahead of the broker's own payment.
    • Track each broker's average days-to-pay: brokers who consistently pay in ~30 days are lower risk than those stretching to 60–90+ days.
    • Concentration risk matters — relying on a single broker is riskier than a spread across many.
    • Grades below reflect your own credit assessment of each broker (A = reliable, fast payer; F = high risk / delinquent) — use them alongside a broker credit check before booking new loads.
